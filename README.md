# Fern Grow

Fern Grow is a blockchain-based ecosystem simulation game where players cultivate virtual forest environments through NFT ownership and collaborative gameplay. Each player starts with a unique procedurally generated seed NFT representing a digital wilderness plot, where individual ecosystems can only thrive through interdependent interactions with neighboring plots owned by other players. Players earn native SPORE tokens by maintaining biodiversity, facilitating cross-pollination, and contributing to the overall health of forest clusters.

The game leverages fractal algorithms to generate infinite ecosystem variations while utilizing layer-2 scaling solutions for micro-transactions related to daily care activities like watering and pest management. Smart contracts automatically distribute rewards based on complex ecosystem health algorithms that consider species diversity, simulated carbon absorption rates, and collaborative care metrics. The platform features dynamically evolving NFT landscapes that change appearance based on seasonal cycles and community-driven environmental events.

Fern Grow creates a sustainable play-to-earn economy where long-term ecosystem thinking generates the highest returns through both individual stewardship and collective environmental goals. Advanced players can establish guild-owned nature reserves and mint rare seed NFTs through successful cross-breeding programs. The game also partners with environmental organizations to trigger real-world tree planting and forest restoration donations based on in-game conservation achievements.
